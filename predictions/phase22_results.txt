  Reading: the family's coherence IS the inherited DQN loop. The delta papers' own contributions (a target rule, a loss, a projection) are one op each
  and do not form a family among themselves. That is not a defect of the family — it is what an incremental lineage is. "Real family" vs "inherited
  structure" is a false dichotomy for delta papers: the mechanism they share is the one they inherit. The honest statement: replay-off-policy is a
  base-loop family with five members, two of which (DQN, DDPG) carry the loop in their own text and three of which carry it by citation.
