READING — why replay-off-policy does not cohere, and why extraction can't fix it
  DQN and DDPG both print the full agent loop as pseudocode (store, sample, select, execute, update). Every later paper in the lineage — DDQN, TD3,
  Rainbow — inherits that loop as background and writes only its delta: DDQN the decoupled select/evaluate, TD3 the clipped target, Rainbow the
  distributional projection. The extractor reads what the paper writes, so the family's shared mechanism never appears in 3 of 5 papers.
  This is a different failure from Phase 19's (wrong section). It is a property of incremental papers: the mechanism a family shares is the one
  its later members don't restate. Lexical says the same thing: replay-off-policy 0.28 vs on-policy 0.50.
  Consequence for the method: mechanism families cohere when members restate the loop (jepa, pc-learning, on-policy trio) and dissolve when members
  are deltas on a base paper. A "delta paper" needs its base paper's loop injected, or scored against the base only.
