TESTABLE TRANSFER HYPOTHESES
  1. (grounded in the 1.00 lexical / sample@sample match with DDIM) Diffuser's planner is the DDPM/DDIM sampler run over tau instead of an image.
     Prediction: substituting DDIM's deterministic sigma=0 sampler with 5-10x fewer denoising steps into Diffuser leaves D4RL return within ~5% of the
     full-step planner, because nothing in Diffuser's method depends on the stochastic Markov reverse chain — only on the learned epsilon_theta(tau, i).
  2. (grounded in the 0.60 lexical match with Differentiable Particle Filters: predict, reconstruct, sample) Diffuser's guided sampling — adding grad J(tau)
     to each denoising step — is a gradient version of importance weighting. Prediction: replacing the gradient guidance with SMC-style resampling of
     N trajectory samples weighted by exp(J(tau)) at each denoising step gives comparable conditioned returns without a differentiable J, at N x cost.
     This is the "probabilistic planning with SMC" structure the gatekeeper first asked for, arrived at from the corpus rather than assumed.
