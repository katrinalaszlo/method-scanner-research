READING
  1. The split the data supports is not policy-gradient vs value-based. It is on-policy trust-region (PPO, TRPO, SAC: evaluate@value + improve@policy/parameter)
     vs replay-based off-policy (DQN, DDPG: store/sample@sample + select/act@action). DDPG is "policy-gradient" by lineage and DQN-loop by mechanism —
     the same lineage-vs-mechanism mismatch as pc-neuro (Phase 15) and A3C (Phase 18), now on the paper the spec assumed would confirm the family.
  2. Three of four new papers got bad input. Section finder: TD3 -> wrong heading; DDQN, Rainbow -> 20% fallback. Rainbow's window landed on the C51
     distributional projection, not the agent loop. Same failure class as Phase 5 (Kalman abstract had no "predict"). Value-based 0.03 is partly an
     extraction result, not a mechanism result. Fix input before judging the family.
  3. value-based has n=4, not 5 — A3C was counted as a member by the spec but is an outlier; effective n=3.
